# Lesson Brief: Rectangle Area from Unit Squares

**Status:** Ready for production-pipeline candidate generation

**Working CoursePack ID:** `rectangle-area-from-tiles`

**Locale:** `zh-CN`

## Natural learner request

> 老师，请给我讲一节课，解释为什么长方形的面积等于长乘宽。我不想只背公式，希望从一平方厘米的小方格铺满长方形开始，弄懂长、宽和方格个数之间的关系，也帮我区分面积和周长。

This request is intentionally written as a normal learner request. It does not
name OLL components, camera behavior, slider controls, layout, or internal
visual capabilities.

## Intended learner

- approximately ages 8–9;
- understands multiplication as equal groups or arrays;
- can count rows and columns;
- is beginning formal study of area;
- may confuse linear units, square units, area, and perimeter.

## Required learning outcomes

By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to:

1. Explain that area measures the amount of a plane region covered by unit squares.
2. Explain why a rectangle with `w` squares in each row and `h` rows contains `w × h` unit squares.
3. Use `area = length × width` to calculate a rectangle's area with correct square units.
4. Distinguish area from perimeter in words and in one nearby example.

## Candidate evaluation rubric

### Course structure

- The lesson separates the meaning of area, the rectangle-rule derivation, and
  the area-versus-perimeter distinction when those are independent teaching goals.
- It does not repeat introductions or compress the entire lesson into one long beat.
- Every narrated beat produces a visible teaching change.

### Mathematical correctness

- Unit squares cover the rectangle without gaps or overlaps.
- Row count and squares-per-row are not reversed in a way that changes the reasoning.
- The numerical example, diagram, formula, and spoken explanation agree.
- Length uses linear units and area uses square units.
- Perimeter is not described as covered space and area is not described as boundary length.

### Visual grounding

- The derivation is shown through a rectangle or array of equal unit squares,
  not only through prose and the final formula.
- The learner can visually connect one side with columns, the other side with
  rows, and the product with the total number of unit squares.
- Supporting formula and note cards remain close to the primary visual without overlap.
- Camera focus excludes unrelated board content and excessive whitespace.

### Interaction

- A meaningful interaction changes a mathematical quantity or asks the learner
  to reach or verify a target; a decorative control does not satisfy this criterion.
- The interaction state, visual state, and task judgement remain consistent.
- If the current generator cannot express tiled-rectangle interaction, that is
  recorded as a shared capability gap instead of being silently hand-authored
  only for this CoursePack.

### Runtime and packaging

- The OLL validates against the current production contract.
- Full playback completes without an RPC or external runtime asset.
- Resume and restart produce deterministic state.
- The layout remains usable in desktop, Android mode, and the physical 4K meeting display.
