# Lesson Brief: Slope and Intercept

**Status:** Ready for production-pipeline candidate generation

**Working CoursePack ID:** `slope-and-intercept`

**Locale:** `zh-CN`

## Natural learner request

> 老师，请用图像给我讲一节课，解释一次函数 y = mx + b 里的 m 和 b 分别有什么作用。我希望能改变 m 和 b，直接看到直线怎样变陡、变缓、改变方向和上下移动，最后学会根据图像判断斜率和纵截距。

This request is intentionally written as a normal learner request. It does not
name OLL components, camera behavior, slider controls, layout, or internal
visual capabilities.

## Intended learner

- approximately ages 12–14;
- understands coordinate axes and ordered pairs;
- can substitute a number into a simple expression;
- is beginning to connect algebraic formulas with graphs;
- may confuse slope with intercept or interpret both as vertical movement.

## Required learning outcomes

By the end of the lesson, the learner should be able to:

1. Identify `m` as slope and `b` as the y-intercept in `y = mx + b`.
2. Explain how the sign and absolute value of `m` affect direction and steepness.
3. Explain that changing `b` translates the line vertically without changing its slope.
4. Read the y-intercept from a graph and use two visible points to interpret slope as vertical change divided by horizontal change.

## Candidate evaluation rubric

### Course structure

- The lesson separates the roles of `m` and `b` before combining them.
- Every section has a distinct teaching goal and every narrated beat produces a visible teaching change.
- The lesson does not collapse all concepts into one long section or repeat the same introduction.

### Mathematical correctness

- Positive, zero, and negative slopes are described correctly.
- A larger absolute slope is described as steeper; a larger signed value is not always called steeper.
- The y-intercept is the value of `y` when `x = 0` and is shown on the y-axis.
- Changing `b` does not change slope, and changing `m` does not move the y-intercept when `b` is fixed.
- Any numerical slope calculation agrees with the visible points and graph.

### Visual grounding

- Formula, controls, and graph remain in one coherent camera-safe region.
- Changes to `m` and `b` are visible on the same graph rather than explained only in prose.
- The graph includes axes and enough scale information to interpret direction, steepness, and intercept.
- Supporting notes stay near the live graph without overlap or excessive whitespace.

### Interaction

- The learner changes both `m` and `b` during the lesson.
- At least one task asks the learner to match a declared target line or declared slope/intercept values.
- Task judgement, slider state, graph state, and narration remain consistent.
- A decorative slider or a hidden target does not satisfy this criterion.

### Runtime and packaging

- The OLL validates against the current production contract.
- Full playback completes without an RPC or external runtime asset.
- Resume and restart produce deterministic state.
- The layout remains usable in desktop, Android mode, and the physical 4K meeting display.
